# Surface brief: index (Grist One landing + purchase)

Scope: single-page DTC product site, Persuade mode. Visitor decides on a ~$250 grinder and buys. Fictional concept; labelled as such in footer and a sample-copy tag on proof.
Audience/job: home-coffee enthusiast checking burr size, clicks, capacity, weight; wants to specify a finish/burr set and buy. Proof: specs page-checkable; sample reviews labelled. Constraints: Vite + vanilla TS, SVG-drawn product, WCAG AA, reduced-motion fallback.
Memorable moment: scroll dismantles the grinder into an exploded inspection drawing; each part is a hotspot with its dimension.
Unresolved: none blocking. Direction chosen by the seeded roll without a user round (user instructed continuous work through Project 01); disclosed in handoff.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Grist sells legibility: the page is an inspection drawing of the object, not lifestyle photography. Refuses the centered-headline-over-product-photo hero, three feature cards, and logo strip.

OWN-WORLD: Anodised Signal Orange ground (#FF4D00) with near-black ink (#111) linework, dimension lines, leader callouts, tolerance boxes and engraved scale ticks; sections change material, not colour scheme: orange sheet, black-anodised graphite (#16181A) for the teardown, bare aluminium grey (#CBD0D2) for specs/comparison. Archivo Variable (heavy, tight, caps for labels) plus Courier Prime only for measured values. Buttons are stamped rectangles with square corners; focus rings are 3px ink offset. No cream, no gradients, no cards; ruled tables and drawing-sheet title blocks.

STORY: Visitor sees a real-scale drawing of the grinder, understands every part is named and dimensioned, believes the claims because each is checkable in the page (click the dial, see 0.02 mm), and acts via a purchase block that stays reachable in a sticky title-block bar.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed orange sheet. Left two-fifths: oversized "GRIST ONE" set flush left, ~6rem, stacked over one dry sentence and the primary "Specify yours — $249" stamp button. Right three-fifths: large SVG side-elevation of the grinder with dimension lines (height, burr diameter 48 mm) and leader callouts. Bottom edge: a drawing title block (scale, material, revision, "CONCEPT — NOT A REAL PRODUCT"). Top: slim nav with a cart counter.

FORM: Engineering inspection drawing sheet (candidate 3 of 7 grounded list: machinist catalogue, patent exploded view, inspection-sheet, instrument manual, surveyor field book, gauge-block case, calibration certificate); seed key 2cfd499c. Signature interaction: scroll-driven exploded teardown plus a rotatable grind dial that converts click count to microns and brew method.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
