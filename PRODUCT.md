# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Vite + vanilla TypeScript, static output. Confirmed by user (2026-09-30). No framework; deployable to any static host. Build path: code-led (no image generation available in this harness).

## Users
Home-coffee enthusiasts (25–45) who already brew pour-over/AeroPress/moka daily, research gear on forums and YouTube, and are deciding whether a ~$250 hand grinder is worth it over a $100 one. Often travellers/campers/desk-workers who value a grinder that is also a good object.

## Product Purpose
Fictional DTC brand **Grist** sells one product: **Grist One**, a machined-aluminium hand coffee grinder. The site must make the object desirable, make its engineering legible, let the visitor specify their own unit, and convert to a purchase. Concept portfolio project; nothing here is a real business.

## Positioning
A grinder sold like a precision instrument: every part is named, dimensioned and explained, and the site lets you take it apart. Competitors sell lifestyle photography; Grist sells legibility.

## Operating Context
Coffee ritual at a kitchen counter, a campsite or a desk. Grind setting is chosen by brew method; buyers compare burr size, clicks-per-setting, capacity, weight, materials, and warranty.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Fictional specs (concept only): 48 mm conical steel burrs, 0.02 mm adjustment per click (external dial), 20 g capacity, 6061 aluminium body, 640 g weight, 168 mm height, 0–60 clicks, two sealed bearings. Specs live in one data file. Finishes: Raw, Anodised Signal Orange, Graphite, Bone. Optional burr set: Filter or Espresso-ready. No photography exists; the product is drawn in SVG. No real testimonials, press, metrics or reviews exist: any social proof is clearly labelled as sample/concept copy.

## Brand Commitments
Name: Grist. Product: Grist One. Voice: plain, exact, dry; explains rather than hypes. Must be presented publicly as a fictional concept project.

## Evidence on Hand
None real. All specs, prices and quotes are invented concept material and labelled as such.

## Product Principles
1. Legibility over lifestyle: show parts, dimensions, and mechanism.
2. The object is the interface: configuring and dismantling the grinder are the main interactions.
3. Claims are specific and checkable in the page itself, never adjectives.
4. Purchase is one clear action, always reachable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard operation of the configurator, parts explorer and cart, reduced-motion fallback to static states.
